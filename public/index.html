    FAQ

    Questions owners ask

    Do you guarantee more followers or sales?

    No. Nobody honest can. What we promise is consistent, good-looking content that goes out on schedule, which is what most small business accounts are missing.

    What do I need to send you?

    Photos and short phone videos of your work, team, products or space, plus any offers or events coming up. We'll tell you exactly what to shoot during kickoff.

    Which platforms do you cover?

    Facebook and Instagram on Growth and Premium. Starter covers one platform of your choice. Video edits are formatted for Reels, TikTok and YouTube Shorts.

    Is there a contract?

    Packages run month to month. Give notice before your next month starts and we stop there.

    Can you film for us?

    Yes, on request. On-site shoots are quoted separately based on location and time.
